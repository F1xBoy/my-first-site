How to get an AI Key?

Option 1: Google Gemini (Free & Recommended)

  1. Go to Google AI Studio.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click the "Create API Key" button.
  4. Copy the generated key and paste it into the VChat settings.

Option 2: OpenAI ChatGPT (Requires paid balance)

  1. Go to the OpenAI Platform.
  2. Sign in and top up your billing balance (minimum $5).
  3. Click "Create new secret key".
  4. Copy the key (it starts with sk-) and paste it into VChat.
🔒 Safe & Private

Your key is saved only on your device (localStorage). It is never sent to our servers, only directly to Google or OpenAI.
